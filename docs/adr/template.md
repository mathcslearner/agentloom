# ADR-NNN: Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-MMM](MMM-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Ticket:** ROADMAP.md ticket X.Y

## Context

What situation forces a decision? Constraints, requirements, and the forces in
tension. Written so a newcomer understands why this couldn't just be deferred.

## Decision

The decision, stated in full sentences and active voice ("We will ..."). Include
enough specifics that implementation can conform to it without guessing.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. Both positive and negative — an
ADR with no downsides listed is under-analyzed.

## Alternatives considered

Each realistic alternative, with the concrete reason it was rejected. This is
what stops the decision from being relitigated from scratch later.
